## Scope

This policy covers refunds, termination, advance payments, and milestone settlements for services of QUBENIX AI PRIVATE LIMITED ("Qubenix", "we", or "us").

The public website is an information and inquiry site. It does not run a self-serve checkout or shopping cart. Engagements are agreed in a written contract.

If a master services agreement, statement of work, or service level agreement sets different payment or termination terms, that signed agreement controls for that project. This page is the baseline where a contract is silent.

## Services covered

- Custom web, mobile, cloud, and database systems
- Applied AI, agents, tool use, and retrieval systems
- Voice agents and telephony automation
- Integrations, middleware, and ERP or CRM automation
- Technical advisory, feasibility reviews, and architecture

These services use dedicated people and custom work, so a refund depends on what has already been done, which milestones are accepted, and which costs are already committed.

## Asking for a refund

1. An authorized representative emails [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in) with the subject "Commercial Refund Request" and the project or agreement reference.
2. The message states the statement of work or invoice, the milestone in question, the reason (for example cancellation before work started, a verified defect, or an agreed reduction in scope), and the relevant correspondence or acceptance notes.
3. Project and finance staff check sprint records, repositories, and signed acceptances.

## Cancellation

Cancel by a written notice to [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in), using the notice period in the contract, or 15 calendar days if the contract does not set one.

After a valid notice we stop further billable work on that project, prepare a statement of work done, deliverables, committed third-party costs, and any unspent balance, and transfer paid deliverables as the contract requires.

## Deposits

Advance payments reserve people, environments, and project-specific quotas. They are not automatically forfeited.

- If you cancel before any engineering, scoping, or provisioning has started, the deposit is refunded minus reasonable administrative and banking fees
- If you cancel after work has started, the deposit is applied to documented time and infrastructure cost up to the notice date. Any unspent, uncommitted balance is refunded

## Work already done

Hours, design, schema, code, and consultations delivered to the agreed specification are not refunded. We keep sprint and repository records so billed work can be checked. You pay for work up to the cancellation notice, plus non-cancelable third-party costs you authorized.

## Milestones

Engagements are split into milestones in the statement of work, such as discovery, a prototype, feature work, acceptance testing, and deployment.

- A milestone that is approved, or treated as accepted when the review window ends, is not refundable
- If a deliverable does not match the statement of work, send a written defect list. We will try to fix it at no extra charge within the agreed remedy window
- If a material defect is not fixed after a fair chance to do so, the parties agree a reduction or refund of that milestone fee

## Changes in scope

Work outside the statement of work needs a written change order or task approval that states the fee and schedule. Fees for additional work follow that change order. Once that work has started on your instruction, the fees incurred for it are not refundable.

## Digital files and training material

Downloaded source, blueprints, and training files cannot be taken back, so they are generally not refundable once delivered, unless the file is corrupt, unreachable, or not what was specified. In that case we provide a corrected file after we confirm the problem.

## Retainers

- End a recurring support or monitoring agreement with at least 30 days' written notice before the next billing cycle
- No later cycle is billed after a timely cancellation
- The fee for the cycle already underway is not refunded, and support continues until that cycle ends

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back by the original method, such as NEFT, RTGS, wire, or the payment gateway, unless another route is agreed in writing. GST is adjusted with a credit note. After written approval, we start the refund within 14 business days. Bank credit depends on the banks involved.

## When a refund is not owed

We are not required to refund where:

- A delay comes from missing credentials, access, requirements, or feedback on your side
- A third-party host or model provider causes downtime or a breaking API change
- Communication stops for more than 60 days without the notice the contract requires
- We end the agreement because of a material breach of intellectual property, confidentiality, or non-solicitation terms

## Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy removes a right under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 that cannot legally be waived.

## Disputes about invoices or refunds

1. Commercial contacts try to resolve the matter within 15 business days of written notice
2. If it is still open after 30 business days, it goes to binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before one arbitrator seated in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, in English
3. Subject to arbitration, the courts of Odisha, India have supervisory jurisdiction

## Contact

Cancellation notices and refund questions go to Commercial Finance and Legal Accounts, QUBENIX AI PRIVATE LIMITED, [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in). We acknowledge inquiries within 48 to 72 business hours.
