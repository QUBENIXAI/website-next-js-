## Introduction

QUBENIX AI PRIVATE LIMITED ("Qubenix", "Company", "we", or "us") provides this page so people whose personal data we process can use their rights, ask about their records, and raise a data-protection complaint.

We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") as its provisions come into force.

## Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we process it, who hosts it, and how it is secured is set out in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## Requests you can make

After we verify your identity, and subject to the limits in Indian law, you may ask us to:

- Confirm whether we hold personal data about you, and summarize it
- Correct or complete inaccurate or outdated information
- Delete data that is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for
- Withdraw consent for voluntary marketing or elective contact
- Register a complaint about how your information was handled

## How to send a request

Privacy requests are handled by email, not through a third-party marketing form. Write to [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in) from the email address connected to the record. Include what you are asking for and enough detail for us to find the record, such as the approximate date of a consultation or a company name.

## Identity

We will not disclose, change, or delete records until we are satisfied the request comes from the person concerned.

- The message should come from the email previously used with that record
- We may ask you to confirm a detail you already gave us, such as a booking date or company
- We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, or a passport for ordinary website inquiries. If a commercial record truly needs stronger proof, that check is done on a separate secure channel

## Corrections

You may ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data. Name the field, send the correct value, and we will update active systems and tell the relevant internal team within the timeline below.

## Deletion

You may ask us to delete personal information when it is no longer needed for the consultation or contract it was collected for, or when you have withdrawn the consent it depended on and no other law requires us to keep it.

If the request qualifies, we delete it from active systems and ask infrastructure providers to do the same, except where a legal retention rule below applies.

## Withdrawing consent

Where we rely only on consent, for example optional updates or a voluntary discovery call, you may withdraw it at any time.

- Email [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in) with the subject "Withdrawal of Consent"
- Use the unsubscribe link in an informational email, if one is present
- For preference cookies, clear them in your browser. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies)

Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

## Other objections

You may opt out of business-development emails at any time. If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, California, or another region with additional rights, we consider requests to restrict processing or to receive a portable copy in line with those rules and our contracts.

## How long we keep data

- Unconverted website inquiries: 12 to 24 months, then reviewed and removed
- Security logs such as IP address and user agent: typically 30 to 90 days
- Invoices, contracts, and tax records: for the period required by company and tax law

## When we may refuse deletion

We may refuse or limit erasure when the law requires us to keep the record, including:

- Invoices and ledgers under the Companies Act, 2013 and GST law
- Records needed for an actual or threatened legal claim
- A lawful order from a court or authority
- Logs needed to investigate a security incident

## Grievances

1. Email [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in) with the subject "Data Protection Grievance – Formal Escalation"
2. Our privacy and legal team reviews the records and sends a written response
3. If you are still not satisfied after that internal process, the DPDP Act allows a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India once that route is open

## Timelines

| Step | Target | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acknowledgment | 48–72 business hours | We confirm receipt and give a reference |
| Verification | 3–5 business days | We check the email and the scope of the request |
| Review | 15 business days | We check systems and any legal reason to retain the data |
| Resolution | 30 calendar days | We correct, delete, or explain, and write to you |

If the request is unusually complex, we will write within the first 30 days and explain the extra time.
