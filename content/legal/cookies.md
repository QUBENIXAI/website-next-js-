## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site sends to your browser. The browser stores them and sends them back on later visits.

Related technologies used by modern sites include:

- Local storage, which keeps key-value data until you or a script clears it
- Session storage, which lasts only while the tab is open
- Service workers, which cache static assets such as fonts and styles so pages load faster

In this policy, "cookies" and "similar technologies" mean all of those mechanisms on this website.

## Why they are used

QUBENIX AI PRIVATE LIMITED ("Qubenix", "we", or "us") uses client-side storage only for operational needs:

- Remembering a language choice so it does not have to be selected on every page
- Remembering a cookie preference so the same prompt is not shown again
- Keeping a local draft of an enquiry so a closed tab or dropped connection does not erase it
- Caching fonts and layout assets for faster pages

## Analytics

Analytics tools measure aggregate use, such as which pages are opened and where delays occur.

None are active. A review of the site found no third-party analytics cookies, including Google Analytics, Adobe Analytics, Matomo, or Microsoft Clarity.

If analytics are added later, they will be optional and off until you opt in.

## Marketing

We do not set marketing cookies, retargeting pixels, or advertising beacons. In particular we do not use the Meta Pixel, the LinkedIn Insight Tag, or Google Ads remarketing tags, and we do not sell browsing profiles.

## Content delivery networks

Pages load libraries and fonts from:

- Cloudflare (`cdnjs.cloudflare.com`), for open-source libraries. Cloudflare may process network data for security. It does not set cross-site tracking cookies for us.
- Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`), for font files. Those requests do not set advertising cookies.

The site does not embed third-party video players or social "like" or "share" scripts that report your visit to those networks.

## Consent

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and comparable rules such as the EU ePrivacy Directive:

- Cookies needed to provide a service you asked for, such as keeping a language you selected, do not need prior consent, but we disclose them here
- Non-essential cookies, including analytics and marketing, need a clear opt-in before they run
- You can accept, reject, or choose by category without losing core access to the site

Phone operating systems also let you reset advertising identifiers. Because this site uses functional and preference storage, turning cookies off in the browser has little effect on reading the pages.
