## Introduction

Welcome to QUBENIX AI PRIVATE LIMITED ("Qubenix", "Company", "we", "us", or "our"). We are an applied artificial intelligence, automation, and enterprise software engineering firm incorporated under the laws of the Republic of India.

We respect the privacy rights of all visitors, clients, partners, and job applicants ("you", "your", or "Data Principal"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, handle, disclose, transfer, and protect your digital personal data when you access our website, interact with our consultation forms, or engage with our technical advisory and custom engineering services.

This policy is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"), and recognizes the governance architecture established by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act, 2023") and rules framed thereunder.

## Company identity and contact

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, the Data Fiduciary determining the purposes and means of processing personal data is QUBENIX AI PRIVATE LIMITED.

- Official website: [https://qubenix.in](https://qubenix.in)
- Policy and privacy contact: [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in)
- Jurisdiction of incorporation: India (Registrar of Companies)

The official Corporate Identification Number (CIN) and registered office address are maintained in verified records with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Government of India. Formal corporate registration filings can be furnished upon written request to [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in).

## Scope of the policy

This Privacy Policy applies to digital personal data collected via:

- Our official website and associated pages
- Consultation booking and project discovery forms
- Communications you send to official Company inboxes, including [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in)
- Technical diagnostic server logs generated during your access to the website

This policy does not govern non-personal data, aggregated operational metrics that cannot be linked to an identifiable individual, enterprise software deployments hosted on client-controlled infrastructure, or third-party platforms linked from our website.

## Information collected through website forms

Personal data is collected when you choose to submit a consultation or project scoping form. We collect only information that is necessary for evaluating technical requirements and responding to your inquiry.

| Field | Requirement | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full name | Mandatory | Used to address you and maintain client communication records. |
| Work email | Mandatory | Used to confirm receipt, deliver proposals, and schedule calls. |
| Company name | Optional | Identifies the organization seeking engineering or AI advisory. |
| Consulting focus area | Mandatory | Specifies the engagement domain, such as AI automation, voice agents, custom software, or system integration. |
| Project goals | Mandatory | Describes operational challenges, the existing software stack, and expected deliverables. |
| Approximate budget | Optional | Helps structure a realistic roadmap and resource plan. |
| Preferred contact method | Mandatory | Indicates whether you prefer email, a video call, or phone. |

For session continuity, form state may be held briefly in your browser's local storage. That copy stays on your device until it is cleared.

## Information you submit voluntarily

You may also share personal information when you:

- Send a careers inquiry, including a CV, portfolio, or code repository, to [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in)
- Ask about partnerships, request a non-disclosure agreement, or submit a technical proposal

## Technical information and server logs

When you access the website, servers and edge networks record standard transmission metadata to keep the site secure and to limit automated attacks:

- IP address, used for routing and denial-of-service protection
- Access date, time, and duration
- Requested address, HTTP status, and referring URL
- Browser, device type, screen size, and operating system

These logs are kept separate from contact submissions and are not used to build identifiable profiles during ordinary operations.

## Cookies and similar technologies

- Essential cookies and security tokens used by delivery networks and firewalls to load pages and prevent cross-site request forgery
- Preference storage used only to keep a language choice or unsent form text
- No third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or behavioral trackers on public pages

You can reject or delete cookies in your browser. Because these cookies are technical, the site remains readable if they are disabled. Details are in the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).

## Purposes of processing

Qubenix processes personal data for defined purposes:

1. Reviewing your business context and technical requirements
2. Preparing architecture proposals, estimates, and quotations
3. Drafting non-disclosure agreements, master service agreements, and statements of work
4. Scheduling calls and answering engineering questions
5. Evaluating applicants and portfolios
6. Detecting unauthorized access, intrusion attempts, and fraud
7. Meeting accounting, tax, and other legal duties under Indian law

## Legal grounds

- Express consent, when you send details through a form or email so we can respond
- Certain legitimate uses, where you provide information for a specified purpose
- Pre-contractual and contractual steps needed to prepare or perform an agreement
- Compliance with law, regulatory orders, or valid judicial process

## How enquiries are handled

Inquiries go to technical leadership and engineering leads. They are reviewed by practicing engineers and architects, not by telemarketing bots or lead brokers. We aim to reply through your chosen channel within 24 business hours. If an engagement does not proceed, the details are archived or deleted under the retention schedule below.

## Hosting and infrastructure

Web servers and static delivery use enterprise cloud infrastructure that follows recognized security frameworks, including providers such as Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, and DigitalOcean. Those providers process data only to host and deliver the service. They are not authorized to sell or independently use your personal data.

## Email and communications

Messages to and from `@qubenix.in` are handled by managed business email services. Transmission uses TLS, and stored mail is encrypted at rest under the provider's commercial terms.

## Analytics

We do not run cross-site trackers or marketing beacons on the public website. Reliability is monitored with aggregate server metrics such as request counts, bandwidth, and latency, which do not follow a person across other sites. If a separate analytics tool is added later, this policy will name it and explain how to opt out.

## AI services and third-party APIs

Personal information submitted through the public website is not shared with public AI models for training.

Where a client contracts us to build custom models, agents, or voice systems using external APIs, those flows are governed by the project agreement, including confidentiality and zero-data-retention terms where the provider supports them.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. It is shared only with:

- Service providers bound by confidentiality and data-protection terms, such as hosting, email, and accounting
- A successor in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, if that party agrees to honor this policy
- Authorities, when a law, court order, or lawful investigation requires it

Because we work with clients in more than one country, information may be processed on secure servers outside your place of residence. Transfers follow applicable Indian law, including restrictions notified under the DPDP Act, and are protected by contract and encryption.

## Retention and deletion

- Enquiries that do not become a client relationship are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymized
- Contract and billing records are kept for the life of the agreement plus the period required by Indian tax and company law
- Resumes are kept for up to 6 months for recruitment, or deleted sooner on request

When the period ends, or a verified erasure request is accepted, the data is removed from operational systems.

## Security

We use HTTPS with current TLS on public endpoints, encryption for data at rest, role-based access, multi-factor authentication, separated development environments, and confidentiality terms for people who handle client information.

## Personal data breaches

We keep an incident process to detect, contain, and investigate security events. If a confirmed breach affects your data, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected people in the manner required by the DPDP Act and CERT-In guidance under the Information Technology Act.

## Your rights

- Access: a summary of the personal data we process and the activities involved
- Correction and erasure: fixing inaccurate data and deleting data that is no longer needed
- Grievance redressal: a way to raise a complaint with us
- Nomination: naming someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to do so

Email [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in) with the subject "Data Principal Rights Request". Describe what you want accessed, corrected, or erased, and write from the email address originally used. We respond to legitimate requests within 30 calendar days. Standard requests are not charged unless they are repetitive or unreasonable.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it by emailing [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in) with the subject "Withdrawal of Consent". We will stop that processing unless the law or an existing contract requires us to continue. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before it.

If you believe someone under 18 has submitted data without a parent's authorization, email [info@qubenix.in](mailto:info@qubenix.in) and we will delete it.
